Privacy Policy
This policy explains how HindTechGroup handles information collected through its public website, enquiries and related online interactions.
Effective date: 12 September 2026

OUR APPROACH
HindTechGroup aims to collect only information reasonably needed for the stated purpose, use it responsibly, protect it with appropriate safeguards, and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary or legally required.
This policy applies to the current public website. A separate notice, consent form, contract or service-specific privacy policy may apply where a particular product, business, application, employment process or customer relationship requires additional information.

1. Information We Collect
Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, company or organisation, enquiry details, CV/resume information and other information you choose to include in a message.
We may also receive limited technical information automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical information, referring page, pages viewed, timestamps, language preferences and basic diagnostic information, where technically available and permitted by law.
Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, medical records, confidential trade secrets or other highly sensitive information through a general contact form unless we specifically request it through an appropriate secure channel.
2. How We Use Information
Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested information, communicate about a potential business relationship, process recruitment enquiries, maintain and secure the website, troubleshoot technical issues, improve website performance and content, prevent misuse or fraud, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights and legitimate business interests where permitted by law.
3. Legal Basis and Consent
Where applicable data-protection law requires a specific legal basis or consent, HindTechGroup will process personal data on that basis. Where consent is relied upon, you may have rights to withdraw consent subject to legal and operational limitations.
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework, personal data processing is subject to applicable statutory requirements, including requirements that may apply to notice, consent, security safeguards and individual rights as the relevant provisions become applicable. The exact obligations can depend on the nature and context of processing.
4. Contact Forms and Email
If you contact us by email, we may retain your message and contact details for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or satisfy legal and accounting requirements.
Do not assume that ordinary email is a fully secure or confidential communication channel. For sensitive matters, we may provide an appropriate alternative communication method.
5. Careers and Recruitment Data
If you submit a CV, resume or job enquiry, we may use the information to assess your application, communicate with you, maintain recruitment records and consider you for relevant opportunities where permitted. Recruitment information may be shared with authorised personnel or service providers who assist with recruitment and administration.
6. Cookies, Local Storage and Similar Technologies
The website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences such as theme selection, security, performance measurement or other legitimate purposes. Third-party services, if added in the future, may use their own cookies or technologies subject to their own policies and any notices or consent requirements that apply.
You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.
7. Analytics
If analytics or performance tools are enabled, they may collect limited usage and technical information to help us understand website performance and improve the user experience. We will configure such services according to applicable legal and contractual requirements.
8. Third-Party Services and Links
The website may link to or use third-party services such as Google Maps, email providers, hosting providers, analytics providers, job platforms or other external services. Once you leave our website or interact directly with a third-party service, that provider's privacy policy and terms may apply.
HindTechGroup does not control third-party privacy practices and cannot guarantee how an external service processes information.
9. Service Providers and Data Sharing
We may use trusted service providers for hosting, website infrastructure, email, security, analytics, recruitment, technical support and other operational functions. Such providers may process information only as reasonably necessary for the services they provide and subject to applicable contractual, security and legal requirements.
We may also disclose information where required by law, lawful government or regulatory request, court order, to establish or defend legal claims, prevent fraud or security abuse, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business restructuring, merger, acquisition or transfer of relevant assets, subject to applicable law.
10. International Data Transfers
Some service providers may process information from locations outside India. Where applicable, we will take steps required by law concerning such processing, transfers, safeguards and contractual arrangements.
11. Data Security
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved. However, no website, internet transmission, email system or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
You are also responsible for protecting your device, browser, account credentials and communications. If you suspect unauthorised use or a security incident involving information you sent to us, please contact us promptly.
12. Data Retention
We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business needs, security, dispute resolution, record keeping and legal or regulatory obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of information.
13. Your Rights and Choices
Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights relating to access to information, correction or updating, withdrawal of consent, erasure/deletion, grievance redressal and other rights available under applicable data-protection law.
Requests can be submitted through our Contact page. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfil a request where a legal exception or retention obligation applies.
14. Children's Privacy
This public corporate website is not intentionally directed at children for the collection of personal information. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes that are unlawful or inappropriate. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.
15. Business-Specific Privacy Notices
HindTechGroup may operate or develop businesses in areas such as software, healthcare/pharma and EV charging technology. A specific product, application, customer contract, employment process or regulated activity may require a more detailed privacy notice and additional safeguards. Where a specific notice applies, it will govern the relevant processing to the extent of any conflict with this general website policy.
16. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technologies, legal obligations or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. You should review this page periodically.
